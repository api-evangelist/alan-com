Two Alans and a question Our company is named after Alan Turing, who laid the mathematical foundations for everything we’re now building on top of, and Alan Watts, who taught that clinging to a fixed identity is the surest way to miss what’s emerging. Both feel relevant right now. Turing’s question, “can machines think?”, has quietly evolved into something more personal: what should we become when machines can think well enough to help?
